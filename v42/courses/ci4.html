The heart of this course

Real Italian for real life, between the warmth of Naples and the sharpness of Milan.

01

Speak from lesson one

Every lesson is a real situation in an Italian city: you order, ask, book and chat, live.

02

One clear grammar step a week

Each unit introduces one structure and puts it straight into conversation.

03

A city as your classroom

Your teacher lives in the city you study, and brings it to your screen.

What you will learn

By lesson 20, you will be able to

  1. Use congiuntivo in context, not just in tests
  2. Follow a fast Italian film without subtitles most of the time
  3. Argue, persuade and negotiate in a small-group setting
  4. Write structured emails, reviews and short articles
  5. Understand regional accents from north to south
  6. Sit and pass an internationally recognised A2 exam
Filmed and taught in Naples

Live from Naples.

Your teacher lives in Naples and teaches from there. The city, its streets and its tables are part of every lesson.

Questions

Before you enrol.

Is this level right for me?

Level 4, Intermediate covers CEFR A2.1 to A2.2. Not sure? Take the free one-minute level check or book a placement call with an advisor.

How do live lessons work?

Each lesson is live on Zoom, once a week, with Luca and a small group of 10 to 12 adults. Every lesson is recorded for you.

What do I receive at the end?

A Club Italia certificate stating your CEFR level, after an oral and written can-do assessment.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the course is not right for you, you are refunded in full within seven days of your first live lesson.
