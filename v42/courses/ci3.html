The heart of this course

Confident daily Italian from Bologna, la Dotta, la Grassa, la Rossa.

01

Speak from lesson one

Every lesson is a real situation in an Italian city: you order, ask, book and chat, live.

02

One clear grammar step a week

Each unit introduces one structure and puts it straight into conversation.

03

A city as your classroom

Your teacher lives in the city you study, and brings it to your screen.

What you will learn

By lesson 20, you will be able to

  1. Move between passato prossimo, imperfetto and trapassato with ease
  2. Express opinion, doubt and preference clearly
  3. Talk about food, cooking, cuisine and regional identity
  4. Understand fast native speech in most everyday situations
  5. Read blogs, restaurant reviews and short essays
  6. Take part in a small group conversation for a full lesson
Filmed and taught in Bologna

Live from Bologna.

Your teacher lives in Bologna and teaches from there. The city, its streets and its tables are part of every lesson.

The full curriculum

20 live lessons, unit by unit.

Five modules of four units. One live lesson a week, each with a clear objective, new language and a real Bologna setting. Open a module to see its units.

Module 1, units 1 to 4Telling storiesBologna and its university, recognising literary past tenses, and building a story in the right order.
  1. 01
    Italy's oldest university, the arcades

    Italy's oldest university, the arcades, the food. Review of A1.2.

  2. 02
    Recognising passato remoto in fairy tales and news

    Recognising passato remoto in fairy tales and news. Passive knowledge only.

  3. 03
    Talking about what happened before what happened

    Talking about what happened before what happened. Storytelling structure.

  4. 04
    The Italian storytelling engine

    The Italian storytelling engine. Real narratives from a Bolognese childhood.

Module 2, units 5 to 8The Italian tableThe food of Bologna, dinner-party conversation, giving opinions, and comparing Italian cities.
  1. 05
    Ragù, tortellini

    Ragù, tortellini, mortadella, Parmigiano. Cooking verbs and cultural vocabulary.

  2. 06
    Dinner-party Italian

    Dinner-party Italian. Interrupting politely, agreeing, disagreeing, refilling wine.

  3. 07
    Expressing opinion

    Expressing opinion: penso che, credo che, secondo me. Congiuntivo introduced softly.

  4. 08
    Comparing places

    Comparing places: più / meno / così… come. Bologna vs Milano vs Roma.

Module 3, units 9 to 12Culture and conversationBooking and describing films, Italian songwriters, the midpoint review, and talking about what could be.
  1. 09
    Reserving tickets, film genres

    Reserving tickets, film genres, describing a plot, recommending.

  2. 10
    Lucio Dalla, Battisti

    Lucio Dalla, Battisti, De Andrè, contemporary indie. Listening lab.

  3. 11
    Midpoint oral assessment, cultural presentation

    Midpoint oral assessment, cultural presentation.

  4. 12
    Hypothetical in the present

    Hypothetical in the present: se + congiuntivo imperfetto + condizionale.

Module 4, units 13 to 16Real-life ItalyNorth, centre and south, work and job interviews, healthcare, and banking and paying bills.
  1. 13
    Describing Italian cities, geography

    Describing Italian cities, geography, north / centre / south differences.

  2. 14
    Vocabulary of work, CV

    Vocabulary of work, CV, colloquio di lavoro, contratti.

  3. 15
    Healthcare vocabulary, at the doctor

    Healthcare vocabulary, at the doctor, at the hospital, the Italian public system.

  4. 16
    Bank vocabulary, opening an account

    Bank vocabulary, opening an account, paying bills, ordering online.

Module 5, units 17 to 20Living like a localRenting a home, Italian holidays and festivals, the dialects, and your A2.1 assessment.
  1. 17
    Renting an apartment, understanding a contract

    Renting an apartment, understanding a contract, negotiating with a landlord.

  2. 18
    Italian holidays

    Italian holidays: Natale, Pasqua, Ferragosto, Palio di Siena, Carnevale di Venezia.

  3. 19
    Introduction to Italian dialects, bolognese

    Introduction to Italian dialects, bolognese, romanesco, napoletano, siciliano, as cultural heritage.

  4. 20
    Full CEFR A2

    Full CEFR A2.1 assessment, oral presentation, path to CI Avanzato.

Questions

Before you enrol.

Is this level right for me?

Level 3, Pre-intermediate covers CEFR A1.2 to A2.1. Not sure? Take the free one-minute level check or book a placement call with an advisor.

How do live lessons work?

Each lesson is live on Zoom, once a week, with Giulia and a small group of 10 to 12 adults. Every lesson is recorded for you.

What do I receive at the end?

A Club Italia certificate stating your CEFR level, after an oral and written can-do assessment.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the course is not right for you, you are refunded in full within seven days of your first live lesson.
