Speak from your very first session: survival Italian, fast.

01

Ten sessions of pure conversation, built on what people really say.

Speak more than your teacher

Small groups mean you talk for most of every session.

03

Real situations

Cafés, trains, markets, phone calls: the Italian you will actually use.

What you will learn

By lesson 10, you will be able to

  1. Greet people and introduce yourself
  2. Order at a café or restaurant
  3. Ask for and understand directions
  4. Shop and handle prices
  5. Talk about your day and your family
  6. Hold a short, simple conversation
Filmed and taught in Venice

Live from Venice.

Your teacher lives in Venice and teaches from there. The city, its streets and its tables are part of every lesson.

The full curriculum

10 live lessons, unit by unit.

Every unit is one live lesson a week with a clear objective, one grammar step, new vocabulary and a real Venice setting. Tap a block to open it.

Units 1 to 2Hello and goodbye to where are you from?
  1. 01Hello and goodbyeGreetings, names, how are you, the first sounds of Italian.
  2. 02Where are you from?Nationalities, cities, numbers one to twenty.
Units 3 to 4At the café to getting around
  1. 03At the caféOrdering coffee and pastries, please and thank you.
  2. 04Getting aroundAsking the way: left, right, straight on, near and far.
Units 5 to 6Shopping to at the restaurant
  1. 05ShoppingPrices, numbers to one hundred, colours and sizes.
  2. 06At the restaurantReading a menu, ordering, asking for the bill.
Units 7 to 8My day to family and friends
  1. 07My dayDaily routine, telling the time, days of the week.
  2. 08Family and friendsDescribing the people in your life.
Units 9 to 10Making plans to your first conversation
  1. 09Making plansInvitations, accepting and saying no politely.
  2. 10Your first conversationA free conversation that pulls the ten sessions together.

Questions

Before you enrol.

Is this level right for me?

Speak Italian, Foundation covers CEFR A0 to A1.1. Not sure? Take the free one-minute level check or book a placement call with an advisor.

How do live lessons work?

Each lesson is live on Zoom, once a week, with Francesca and a small group of 10 to 12 adults. Every lesson is recorded for you.

What do I receive at the end?

A Club Italia certificate stating your CEFR level, after an oral and written can-do assessment.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the course is not right for you, you are refunded in full within seven days of your first live lesson.
