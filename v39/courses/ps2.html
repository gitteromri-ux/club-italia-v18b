The heart of this course

The practical situations of daily Italian life, without a script.

01

No grammar drills

Ten sessions of pure conversation, built on what people really say.

02

Speak more than your teacher

03

Real situations

Cafés, trains, markets, phone calls: the Italian you will actually use.

What you will learn

By lesson 10, you will be able to

  1. Talk about your work and free time
  2. Travel by train and stay in a hotel
  3. Book by phone with confidence
  4. Describe last weekend in the past tense
  5. Explain a problem and ask for help
  6. Tell a short story in Italian
Filmed and taught in Milan

Live from Milan.

Your teacher lives in Milan and teaches from there. The city, its streets and its tables are part of every lesson.

The full curriculum

10 live lessons, unit by unit.

Every unit is one live lesson a week with a clear objective, one grammar step, new vocabulary and a real Milan setting. Tap a block to open it.

Units 1 to 2Talking about work to free time
  1. 01Talking about workWhat you do, where and how you work.
  2. 02Free timeHobbies, sport and what you like doing.
Units 3 to 4At the market to travelling by train
  1. 03At the marketQuantities, fresh food and bargaining politely.
  2. 04Travelling by trainTickets, timetables, platforms and delays.
Units 5 to 6At the hotel to on the phone
  1. 05At the hotelChecking in, requests and small problems.
  2. 06On the phoneBooking a table, a tour or an appointment.

  1. 07Last weekendYour first steps in the past tense.
  2. 08At the pharmacyExplaining how you feel and asking for help.
Units 9 to 10Shopping for clothes to tell me a story
  1. 09Shopping for clothesTrying on, comparing and giving opinions.

Questions

Before you enrol.

Is this level right for me?

Speak Italian, Beginner covers CEFR A1.1 to A1.2. Not sure? Take the free one-minute level check or book a placement call with an advisor.

How do live lessons work?

Each lesson is live on Zoom, once a week, with Alessandro and a small group of 10 to 12 adults. Every lesson is recorded for you.

What do I receive at the end?

A Club Italia certificate stating your CEFR level, after an oral and written can-do assessment.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the course is not right for you, you are refunded in full within seven days of your first live lesson.
