A Structured, CEFR-Aligned Curriculum

A curriculum designed by Italian language specialists, aligned with the Common European Framework.

Action-oriented, communicative learning, aligned with the Common European Framework (CEFR). You don’t study Italian. You act in it.

01 Learning by doing

You act, you speak

A social agent acts with intention, ordering, asking, negotiating. Every lesson moves you toward doing something real in Italian.

02 Situated learning

Learned where it’s lived

Every unit is anchored to a real Italian place. Small live groups of 10–12 built for interaction, not lectures.

03 Culture at the heart

Culture at the core

CEFR-aligned (A0 to A2.2) with CILS and CELI style can-do assessment, cultural fluency and measurable progress, together.
