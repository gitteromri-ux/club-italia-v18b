The heart of this course

Everyday Italian from Florence, the city where the language was standardised.

01

Speak from lesson one

Every lesson is a real situation in an Italian city: you order, ask, book and chat, live.

02

One clear grammar step a week

Each unit introduces one structure and puts it straight into conversation.

03

A city as your classroom

Your teacher lives in the city you study, and brings it to your screen.

What you will learn

By lesson 20, you will be able to

  1. Narrate a past event or weekend in detail
  2. Describe people, places and things with precision
  3. Make and change plans on the phone
  4. Handle emails and short letters in Italian
  5. Understand a Tuscan waiter, shopkeeper or landlord
  6. Read simple newspaper headlines and short articles
Filmed and taught in Florence

Live from Florence.

Your teacher lives in Florence and teaches from there. The city, its streets and its tables are part of every lesson.

The full curriculum

20 live lessons, unit by unit.

Every unit is a live, 85-minute lesson with a clear objective, one grammar step, new vocabulary and a real Florence setting. Tap a block to open it.

Units 1 to 4Why Italian is Tuscan to describing people physically and in character
  1. 01Why Italian is TuscanWhy Italian is Tuscan. Dante, Boccaccio, Petrarch. Review of A1.1.
  2. 02Passato prossimo consolidationPassato prossimo consolidation, time markers (ieri, la settimana scorsa).
  3. 03Imperfetto for description and habitImperfetto for description and habit, comparison with passato prossimo.
  4. 04Describing people physically and in characterDescribing people physically and in character. Adjective agreement in depth.
Units 5 to 8Rooms to weights
  1. 05RoomsRooms, prepositions of place, ci per luogo. A Florentine apartment tour.
  2. 06Regional food vocabularyRegional food vocabulary, ne partitivo, cooking verbs. Ribollita, bistecca, cantucci.
  3. 07Full dining dialogueFull dining dialogue, complaining politely, tipping, il conto per favore.
  4. 08WeightsWeights, containers, packaging. Mercato Centrale.
Units 9 to 12Phone conventions to reflexive verbs
  1. 09Phone conventionsPhone conventions, formal register, leaving a message.
  2. 10Making appointmentsMaking appointments, doctor, hairdresser, notary. The Italian bureaucratic register.
  3. 11Body vocabularyBody vocabulary, at the pharmacy, common ailments, mi fa male + body part.
  4. 12Reflexive verbsReflexive verbs, morning routine, adverbs of frequency.
Units 13 to 16Hobbies to simple future tense
  1. 13HobbiesHobbies, sport, Serie A football, Sunday papers.
  2. 14Fellini and SorrentinoFellini and Sorrentino, film vocabulary, expressing opinion.
  3. 15Midpoint reviewMidpoint review, oral test, cultural quiz on Florence.
  4. 16Simple future tenseSimple future tense, plans, promises, predictions.
Units 17 to 20Introduction to hypothetical to cefr a1
  1. 17Introduction to hypotheticalIntroduction to hypothetical: se + present / future, se + imperfetto / condizionale (light touch).
  2. 18Condizionale for polite requests and wishesCondizionale for polite requests and wishes: vorrei, mi piacerebbe, potrei.
  3. 19Email conventionsEmail conventions, formal vs informal register, greetings and sign-offs.
  4. 20CEFR A1CEFR A1.2 full assessment, cultural presentation, path to CI Intermedio.

Questions

Before you enrol.

Is this level right for me?

Level 2, Elementary covers CEFR A1.1 to A1.2. Not sure? Take the free one-minute level check or book a placement call with an advisor.

How do live lessons work?

Each lesson is live on Zoom, 85 minutes, with Chiara and a small group of 10 to 12 adults. Every lesson is recorded for you.

What do I receive at the end?

A Club Italia certificate stating your CEFR level, after an oral and written can-do assessment.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the course is not right for you, you are refunded in full within seven days of your first live lesson.
