The heart of this course

The practical situations of daily Italian life, without a script.

01

No grammar drills

Ten sessions of pure conversation, built on what people really say.

02

Speak more than your teacher

03

Real situations

Cafés, trains, markets, phone calls: the Italian you will actually use.

What you will learn

By lesson 10, you will be able to

  • ITalk about your work and free time
  • IITravel by train and stay in a hotel
  • IIIBook by phone with confidence
  • IVDescribe last weekend in the past tense
  • VExplain a problem and ask for help
  • VITell a short story in Italian

The full curriculum

10 live lessons, unit by unit.

Every unit is one live lesson a week with a clear objective, one grammar step, new vocabulary and a real Milan setting. Tap a block to open it.

Units 1 to 2Talking about work to free time

  1. 01

    Talking about work

    What you do, where and how you work.

  2. 02

    Free time

    Hobbies, sport and what you like doing.

Units 3 to 4At the market to travelling by train

  1. 03

    At the market

    Quantities, fresh food and bargaining politely.

  2. 04

    Travelling by train

    Tickets, timetables, platforms and delays.

Units 5 to 6At the hotel to on the phone

  1. 05

    At the hotel

    Checking in, requests and small problems.

  2. 06

    On the phone

    Booking a table, a tour or an appointment.

Units 7 to 8Last weekend to at the pharmacy

  1. 07

    Last weekend

    Your first steps in the past tense.

  2. 08

    At the pharmacy

    Explaining how you feel and asking for help.

Units 9 to 10Shopping for clothes to tell me a story

  1. 09

    Shopping for clothes

    Trying on, comparing and giving opinions.

  2. 10

    Tell me a story

    A short story about a trip, told in Italian.
